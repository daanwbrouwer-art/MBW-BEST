2. Information we collect

What we collect depends on how you use the App. Guests who never create an account generate the least data (most guest data — onboarding answers, workout history, streaks — stays only on your device, in local app storage, and is never sent to us).

2.1 Account information

  • Username — chosen at signup, shown to other users in Nearby Athletes, Chat, and leaderboards where applicable.
  • Email address — used to create and secure your account, for sign-in, and for account-related communications (password resets, purchase confirmations, service notices).
  • Password — stored only as a securely hashed value by our authentication provider, Supabase; we do not store or have access to your plaintext password.

2.2 Health and fitness information you provide

During onboarding and in your profile, you may provide: gender, age, weight, and height. We use these to estimate calories burned and tailor workout difficulty and recommendations — including a Body Mass Index (BMI)-style calculation used only for this purpose. You also tell us your equipment access (e.g. resistance bands, rings, pull-up bar) and fitness level/goals, used the same way. Providing this information is optional beyond what's required to use core features; declining may limit personalization.

2.3 Workout and activity data

Cards drawn, exercises completed, reps, calories burned, workout duration, streaks, weekly challenge results, and unlocked achievements — generated as you use the App, used to show your history and progress and to power streaks/achievements.

2.4 Location information

If you grant location permission, the App accesses your device's precise location to: show nearby calisthenics parks on the Map, and (if you opt in) show you to other users in Nearby Athletes and let you find them. Nearby Athletes visibility is off by default; if you turn it on, we share only a rounded, approximate location (roughly 100 meters) and your last-active time with other users — never your exact coordinates. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device's system settings; doing so disables the Map and Nearby Athletes features but does not affect the rest of the App.

2.5 Camera and photos

If you submit a photo of a calisthenics park (via your device's camera or photo library, using the App's camera integration), we upload that photo to our storage provider and associate it with the park listing you're contributing to. We only access your camera or photo library when you actively choose to take or select a photo for this purpose — the App does not access your camera or photo library in the background.

2.6 Communications with other users

If you use Chat (available once you've connected with someone via Nearby Athletes), we store the messages you send and receive so the conversation can be displayed to both participants. We also store block and report actions you take, to enforce them and review reported conduct.

2.7 Subscription and purchase information

If you subscribe, our subscription-management provider, RevenueCat, and the App Store or Google Play (whichever store processed your purchase) tell us your subscription status, plan, and renewal/expiration dates, so the App can unlock paid features. We never receive, process, or store your payment card or bank details — those are handled entirely by Apple or Google. See our Subscription Terms for full billing details.

2.8 Device and usage information

Standard technical information any app collects to function and stay reliable: device type and operating system, app version, and basic diagnostic/crash information.

3. How we use this information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's features (workouts, streaks, achievements, Map, Nearby Athletes, Chat, Train Together, subscriptions).
  • To personalize workout difficulty and recommendations based on the fitness information you provide.
  • To secure your account and the App, including detecting and preventing abuse, fraud, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To communicate with you about your account, purchases, and material changes to our services or policies.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests.

We do not use your health or fitness information, or your location, for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

5. Data retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see Section 6.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal information: access, correction, deletion, portability, objection to or restriction of processing, and — where we rely on your consent (such as location or camera access) — withdrawal of that consent at any time via your device settings. To exercise any of these rights, contact hello@my-bodyweight.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

If you reside in the UK or European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

7. Children's data

The App is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16 (or the minimum digital-consent age in your country, if higher) without a parent's or guardian's permission, consistent with our Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures (including encryption in transit, access controls, and row-level security on our database) to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International data transfers

Your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than the one you live in, including by our infrastructure providers. Where we transfer personal information out of the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on recognized transfer mechanisms such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or an applicable adequacy decision.

10. Managing permissions

Location and camera/photo access are both optional and controlled through your device's operating-system settings (iOS: Settings → MyBodyWeight; Android: Settings → Apps → MyBodyWeight → Permissions). Denying or later revoking either permission only disables the specific features that depend on it (Map/Nearby Athletes for location; park-photo submission for camera/photos) — the rest of the App continues to work normally.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal or operational reasons. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice through the App or by email as required by applicable law.
