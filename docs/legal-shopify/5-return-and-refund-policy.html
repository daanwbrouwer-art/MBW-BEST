MyBodyWeight VOF sells digital subscriptions to the MyBodyWeight app. Subscriptions are purchased and paid through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not sell products through our website.

Right of withdrawal (EU and UK consumers)

You may withdraw from a subscription within 14 days of your first payment, without giving a reason. Because the service starts immediately at your request, you pay only for the part of the service already provided and receive a refund for the rest. A free trial can be cancelled at any time without charge.

How to request a refund

  • Google Play: Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history (Order history) → select the purchase → Request a refund.
  • Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Or email us at mybodyweight@outlook.com with your purchase date and account email. We will arrange the refund within 14 days.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time in Google Play or the App Store, or in the app under Profile → Manage or cancel subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Where the law of your country gives you the right to end a renewed subscription early, we refund the unused part.
