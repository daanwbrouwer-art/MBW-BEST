2. The short version

  • We collect only what the App needs to work. We do not show ads, we do not sell your personal data, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Your body measurements (age, height, weight, BMI) are stored only on your device.

3. What data we process and why

For each category below we state the purpose and the legal basis under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

3.1 Account data

Username, email address, and password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider; we never see your password), account identifiers, and the date you created your account.

Purpose: creating and securing your account, signing in, password resets, and account and service messages. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

3.2 Profile and preference data

Your gender, fitness level, equipment you have access to, weekly goals, messaging preference (for example "same gender only"), visibility setting, notification settings, and similar App preferences.

Purpose: selecting the right decks and exercises, applying your chat and visibility preferences, and personalizing the App. Gender is stored in your account because other users' chat and visibility settings depend on it. Legal basis: performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

3.3 Body measurements (health-related data)

Age, height, weight, and the BMI-style value calculated from them. These are used to estimate calories burned and to tailor workout difficulty.

This data is stored only on your device and is not sent to us. It is optional; leaving it out only makes calorie estimates less personal. Because this information can reveal something about your health, if we ever offer to sync or back it up to your account, we will ask for your explicit consent first (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), and you will be able to withdraw that consent at any time.

3.4 Workout and activity data

Workouts you complete: deck, cards and exercises, reps, hold times, duration, estimated calories, streaks, weekly challenges, achievements, and Train Together results. For guests this stays on the device. For account holders, workout history and lifetime statistics are stored in your account so they survive reinstalls and device changes.

Purpose: showing your history and progress, streaks, achievements, challenges, and multiplayer sessions. Legal basis: performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)). We treat this as ordinary fitness data, not medical data: it records exercise, not symptoms or medical conditions, and it is never used for advertising or profiling.

3.5 Location

  • On your device: when you allow location access, the App uses your precise device location to center the map and show calisthenics parks near you. This is processed on your device; for this purpose we do not store it.
  • Nearby Athletes (opt-in, off by default): only if you turn on "Visible to nearby athletes" do we store your location in your account, rounded to about 100 meters, together with your last-active time. Other users then see your username and approximate distance, never your exact position. You can turn this off at any time in your Profile; turning it off erases the stored location.
  • Place search: if you type a place name into the map search, that text is sent to the OpenStreetMap Foundation's geocoding service to find it.
  • Map tiles: to draw the map, your device downloads map images from our map-tile provider, which therefore receives your IP address and the map area being viewed.

Legal basis: your consent, given through the device's location permission and, for Nearby Athletes, through the visibility switch (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and Art. 11.7a of the Dutch Telecommunications Act for access to your device). You can withdraw consent at any time in your device settings or with the in-app switch. The App does not access your location in the background.

3.6 Social features: chat, friends, Train Together, and reports

Messages you send and receive, friend and invitation requests, Train Together session data, and users you block or report (including the reason you give).

Purpose: delivering messages and multiplayer sessions, and keeping the community safe. Legal basis: performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) for delivering the features; our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and enforcing our Terms of Service (Art. 6(1)(f)) for blocks and reports. Chats are not end-to-end encrypted. We do not read them routinely, but we may review the relevant messages when a user reports them, or when the law requires us to.

3.7 Parks and photos you contribute

Park listings (name, location, equipment) and photos you submit, including photos taken with your camera or picked from your gallery. The App opens the camera or gallery only when you choose to add a photo. Approved parks and their photos are visible to all App users. You can add parks without an account; we then create an anonymous technical identifier so the submission can be stored and moderated.

Purpose: building the community park map, and moderating submissions. Legal basis: performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in moderation (Art. 6(1)(f)). Do not include people's faces or other personal details in park photos.

3.8 Subscriptions and purchases

Your subscription status, plan, trial and renewal dates, and an anonymous purchase identifier, received from Google Play or the Apple App Store through our subscription provider RevenueCat. We never receive your card or bank details; payments are handled entirely by Google or Apple. We may also link your account to a Founding Member waitlist entry that uses the same email address, so that we can honor that offer.

Purpose: unlocking paid features, restoring purchases, and honoring promotions. Legal basis: performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); keeping transaction records required by tax law (Art. 6(1)(c)).

3.9 Technical and security data

When the App communicates with our servers, our hosting providers automatically record technical data such as IP address, request time, device and operating-system type, and error information in server logs. Google Play and Apple may also give us aggregated, anonymized crash and performance statistics. Basic App state (such as your settings and offline progress) is saved in local storage on your device so the App works. This storage is strictly necessary for the App to function and is not used for tracking.

Purpose: operating, securing, and fixing the App. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a secure and working service (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3.10 Communications

If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you. We send service emails such as account confirmations, password resets, and important changes. We send marketing emails only if you have asked for them (for example by joining our waitlist), and every marketing email lets you unsubscribe. Legal basis: performance of contract for service emails; consent for marketing (Art. 6(1)(a)).

3.11 Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App shows reminders (such as streak and workout reminders) that it generates on your device. You can turn them off in your Profile or device settings.

3.12 What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not use your data for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you (Art. 22 GDPR).
  • We do not collect contacts, call logs, SMS, or your device's advertising ID.

If we add a feature that uses personal data in a new way, we will update this policy before launching it, and where required we will ask your permission in the App first.

7. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time:

  • In the App: Profile → Legal → Delete Account.
  • On the web, no app needed: daanwbrouwer-art.github.io/MBW-BEST/delete-account.
  • By email: write to hello@my-bodyweight.com from your account's email address. We complete requests within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Deletion removes your account, profile, workout history, location, chats, friends, Train Together data, blocks and reports, park photos, and any waitlist entry from our live systems immediately. Deleted data disappears from backups within 30 days. We keep only what Section 6 says must stay (purchase and tax records, and parks that are no longer linked to you). Data on your device is wiped when you delete in the App; if you delete on the web, uninstall the App or clear its data to remove the local copy too. If you added parks as a guest without an account, email us to have those contributions removed.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play or the App Store (see our Subscription Terms).

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict processing;
  • data portability: receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before.

Most settings can be changed in the App itself. For anything else, email hello@my-bodyweight.com. We respond within one month, and may ask you to confirm your identity first. Exercising your rights is free.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl); you can also contact the authority in the EU country where you live or work. We would appreciate the chance to solve the problem first.

Wherever you live, we extend these rights to you. If the privacy laws of your country (for example the UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws) give you additional rights, you can exercise them through the same contact. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those laws define those terms.

9. Age requirement

The App is intended only for people aged 16 and over, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If we learn that a user is under 16, we will delete their account. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us.

10. Security

We protect your data with technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encryption at rest on our database provider;
  • row-level access rules, so users can only reach their own data;
  • hashed passwords;
  • access limited to the founders who need it, protected by strong authentication;
  • coarsening of shared locations.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach is likely to put you at high risk, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
